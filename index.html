Frequently asked questions.

No. The site is being positioned as a broader digital platform. Educational and training features can be added without changing that identity.

Can it later have applications and student accounts?

Yes. The current structure leaves room for applications, accounts, private dashboards, certificates and other future services.

Can the website work well on a phone?

Yes. The design is mobile-first and can be expanded for larger screens too.
